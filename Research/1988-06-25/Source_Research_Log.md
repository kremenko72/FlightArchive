# Source Research Log — 25 June 1988 (SFR Yugoslavia)

**FlightArchive research case:** 25 June 1988  
**Status:** Initial discovery / not yet flight reconstruction  
**Created:** 23 November 2026

## Research objective
Identify and catalogue evidence that can support a historically grounded reconstruction of air traffic over SFR Yugoslavia on 25 June 1988. This log distinguishes confirmed source availability from conclusions about actual flights.

## Initial discoveries

### 1. Official Airline Guide (OAG), June 1988
- A June 1988 Worldwide Edition of the Official Airline Guide was identified in an archival record / PDF search result.
- This is a promising period source for scheduled routes, flight numbers, times, and airport connections.
- Source record: https://s3.amazonaws.com/NARAprodstorage/lz/electronic-records/rg-197/DB23T/128.1DP.pdf
- Status: **Lead identified; contents and relevant Yugoslav pages not yet extracted or verified.**
- Caution: schedule publication does not prove a flight operated on the target date.

### 2. JAT timetable archive — AirTimes
- AirTimes' JAT timetable index lists historical timetable publication periods and an image gallery.
- The index currently lists JAT schedules covering 29 Oct 1987–26 Mar 1988 and 30 Oct 1988–25 Mar 1989, but does not list a JAT system timetable covering the target summer 1988 period in this index.
- Source: https://www.airtimes.com/cgat/yu/jat.htm
- JAT 1980s gallery: https://www.airtimes.com/cgat/yu/jat/gal/jugal1a80.htm
- Status: **Archive identified; target-season JAT timetable remains a gap in this index.** Other collections or original material may fill it.

### 3. TimetableImages — JAT collection
- A JAT timetable image archive exists with historical material, but the indexed dates reviewed do not show the summer 1988 timetable.
- Source: https://timetableimages.com/ttimages/ju.htm
- Status: **Archive identified; target date/season not confirmed.**

### 4. Northwestern University Transportation Library — George M. Foster Timetable Collection
- A catalogue record for JAT exists within the George M. Foster Timetable Collection, comprising digitized airline timetables across an international collection spanning 1929–1991.
- Source record: https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/5/archival_objects/467395
- Status: **Potential archival avenue; specific 1988 timetable item not yet identified.**

### 5. Yugoslav airport context (annual, not daily)
- A European Communities report contains a table of Yugoslav airports and 1988 annual flight/passenger statistics, citing the Yugoslav Federal Statistical Office's Statistical Bulletin 1672, *Traffic and Communications*, published 1990.
- Source: https://aei.pitt.edu/41739/1/A5922.pdf
- Status: **Contextual evidence only; annual totals do not establish traffic on 25 June 1988.**

## Preliminary airport scope
Candidate airports to investigate include Belgrade/Surčin, Zagreb/Pleso, Ljubljana/Brnik, Sarajevo/Butmir, Split/Resnik, Dubrovnik/Čilipi, Skopje/Petrovec, Titograd/Golubovci, Priština/Slatina, Rijeka/Omišalj, Pula, Zadar, Tivat, Ohrid, Osijek, Niš, Maribor and Mostar. This is a preliminary list, not a confirmed set of operating airports or flights on the target date.

## Evidence discipline
- Timetable = evidence of a scheduled service, not proof of operation.
- Annual airport statistics = context, not daily traffic evidence.
- Every observation should preserve source, page/item, validity period, time zone if available, and confidence.
- Do not invent flight operations, aircraft registrations, routes, or positions.
